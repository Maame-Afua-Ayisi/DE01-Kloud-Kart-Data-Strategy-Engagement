## Programme-wide measures of success

- **Website performance:** Reporting no longer slows down the live website.
- **Consistent revenue:** Finance and Operations use the same agreed revenue figures.
- **Efficient reporting:** Daily reports replace manual CSV processing.
- **Reliable stock management:** Live stock decisions remain accurate and use the operational database.
- **Early issue detection:** Stock discrepancies are identified before they cause overselling.
- **Data protection:** Customer information, access, exports and deletion requests are properly controlled.
- **Clear accountability:** The Data Engineer and Analytics Engineer/Analyst fulfil their defined responsibilities.
- **Evidence-based growth:** Further investment in advanced analytics or infrastructure is justified by measurable business value.

**Roadmap principle:** Each quarter must deliver a measurable business improvement that Nana can independently

verify. Completing technical tasks alone is not success; resolving the underlying business problems is.
